# Raghav Soni &mdash; Personal Portfolio Website

A clean, formal, recruiter-friendly personal portfolio website for **Raghav Soni**, a second-year **Computer Science &amp; Engineering** undergraduate student at **Lakshmi Narain College of Technology &amp; Science (LNCTS), Bhopal** (affiliated with **RGPV**).

## 🎨 Design & Aesthetic: White + Navy Blue

- **Color Palette**:
  - **White Background**: `#FFFFFF` for clear, readable primary presentation and generous whitespace.
  - **Navy Blue Headings & Navigation**: `#0F172A` (Deep Slate Navy) for strong contrast and executive hierarchy.
  - **Primary Blue Accents**: `#2563EB` (Hover: `#1D4ED8`) for purposeful CTA buttons, tags, and interactive links.
  - **Alternate Backgrounds & Card Fills**: `#F1F5F9` / `#F8FAFC` for subtle visual depth and section separation.
  - **Slate Grey Body Text**: `#475569` for effortless long-form readability.
  - **Subtle Borders**: `#E2E8F0` for delicate containment without distracting outlines.
- **Minimal, Professional Résumé Style**: Designed for recruiters, faculty, and technical peers—eliminating dark neon gradients, glow effects, or distracting animations in favor of polished typography and structure.
- **Candidate Overview Card**: Clean executive profile card displaying verified college affiliation, graduation timeline, CGPA, and focus areas.
- **Fully Responsive**: Adapts seamlessly to mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
- **Zero Heavy Dependencies**: Pure semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript (ES6+).
